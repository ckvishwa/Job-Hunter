## Next three engineering tickets (recommended order)

1. **SDLC-T01 — Minimal CI.** Smallest change that makes "tests passed" a checked, reproducible claim on every push, and the prerequisite for protection and scanning. Requires only the owner's decision on the integration branch (C4) to target.
2. **SDLC-T04 — Secret/PII scan.** The repository is public and the product handles private candidate data; the scan is the cheapest control against the highest-impact failure. Pair with the owner enabling GitHub secret scanning and push protection.
3. **V1-T01 — Submit-guard negative tests.** The Enter/Next/upload-auto-submit guards exist in `src/pipeline/application-form.ts` but no test exercises them, and "zero unapproved submission" is the product's central safety claim.

Not in the next three on purpose: any SaaS lane work, the V1 live-form work (needs human authorization and data), and `SDLC-T02` (branch protection should wait until CI has proven reliable).

## Definition of ready for any ticket

Bounded scope and owner; requirement IDs; acceptance criteria that name an independent oracle and the test layer; files it may touch; explicit statement that it does not submit applications, bypass verification, or touch private candidate data; for human steps, the exact decision requested.
